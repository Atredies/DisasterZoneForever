# DisasterZone 0.2.7 for WoW Forever (guild invites fixed)

An update for **&lt;Casual Disaster&gt;**'s guild addon on **WoW Forever** (client 1.60.1, Interface 16001).

**Already on 0.2.3 or newer? You don't need this download.** 0.2.7 reaches you in game: type **`/dz update`**.
This zip is for anyone installing fresh or who can't get the update in game. It is 0.2.3 with in-game update #4
already in it, so later in-game updates keep reaching you too.

## Install / update

Step-by-step guide with pictures of the folder layout:
**[README](https://github.com/Atredies/DisasterZoneForever#readme)**. In short:

1. Download **`DisasterZone-0.2.7.zip`** below (under *Assets*).
2. Close the game.
3. Delete the old `DisasterZone*` folders from `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`, then unzip the new zip
   there, so the `DisasterZone` folder sits directly in `AddOns` (`...\AddOns\DisasterZone\DisasterZone.toc`).
4. Start the game. On the character screen, check that DisasterZone is ticked under **AddOns**. In game,
   `/dz version` shows which version everyone runs.

Your settings, BiS lists, recruitment messages and game progress are kept (they live in your saved variables, not in
these folders).

## What changed since 0.2.6

- **Invite to the guild works again.** On Forever it got DisasterZone blocked ("an action only available to the
  Blizzard UI"). The invite dialog's button now sends the game's own `/ginvite`. The reminder for someone who came
  online opens the same dialog.

## In 0.2.6 (since 0.2.3)

- **Recruitment.** The Whisper buttons work with Forever's two-part names. Anyone who whispers you within 2 hours of a
  recruitment post shows up under People > Talking. Icon parts send a text marker (`>>`, `~*~` ...), since Forever's
  chat draws no raid icons. The GM, officers and allowed raid leaders share the same messages and places, and each
  place sends its own message (the line under its name on Posting).
- **Raid times: Local | Server.** A switch on the Raids tab and in Settings > Window shows every raid day and time in
  your own clock or the server's.
- **Busy calendar days turn in pages** (`< 1/2 >` or the mouse wheel), so every raid of the day can be opened.
- **Bingo:** click a player in the race to see their card; "My card" takes you back.
- **Connected realms:** members from the guild's other connected realms share its data and Discord sign-ups.
- **Discord:** answering a locked raid sends a request to join. **Chess:** ladder results are only taken from the
  player you asked.
- 0.2.4 and 0.2.5 (in game only): guildmates with DisasterZone show right in the roster, and the Guest Book's
  right-click entries work again.

## Something wrong?

Report it in game: **`/dz bug`** (the last Lua error goes with it), **`/dz idea`** for ideas, **`/dz feedback`** to see
every report and its answers. Say what you were doing and where.
