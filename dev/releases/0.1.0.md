# DisasterZone 0.1.0 for WoW Forever (first test build)

The first test build of **&lt;Casual Disaster&gt;**'s guild addon for **WoW Forever** (client 1.60.1, Interface 16001).
Expect rough edges, and please report what breaks (see below).

## Install

1. Download **`DisasterZone-0.1.0.zip`** below (under *Assets*).
2. Close the game. Unzip into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`, so the `DisasterZone` folder sits
   directly in `AddOns` (`...\AddOns\DisasterZone\DisasterZone.toc`).
3. Start the game again. On the character screen, check that DisasterZone is ticked under **AddOns**. In game,
   `/dz` opens it.

The zip holds:

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `DisasterZone` | the addon (required) |
| `DisasterZone_Rooms` | the chess hall's 3D board (needed by Chess) |
| `DisasterZone_Bingo` | Council Bingo |
| `DisasterZone_Azeromon` | Azeromon |
| `DisasterZone_HoldTheLine` | Hold the Line |
| `DisasterZone_Chess` | Karazhan Chess |

The games are optional: leave out the folders you don't want.

**Updating later:** delete the old `DisasterZone*` folders first, then unzip the new zip. Settings and game progress
are kept (they live in your saved variables, not in these folders).

## What works

- Raid planner for Forever's raids: Barrow Deeps (10), Hyjal Summit (20) and Onyxia's Lair (40), level 60.
- Loot council, My BiS and the item picker with Forever's items (dungeon, crafted, reputation and PvP gear). Gear tiers
  are seasons (Pre-raid, Season 1). Raid loot comes once the raids open on 9 December.
- The Atlas with Forever's 35 dungeons, crafting, reputation and PvP.
- The Groups board: dungeons by level band, PvP battlegrounds.
- The members roster and the guild sync. Names with surnames work everywhere: the roster, raids, loot and the guild
  master.
- Paladin blessings and the shaman totem planner, with Forever's spells.
- Games: Bingo, Chess, Azeromon and Hold the Line.
- Addon messages wait out a boss pull (Forever holds them while you fight) and arrive after.

## Hidden on Forever (for now)

- Gear sim, My Log, the strategy boards, meters and boss mods: Forever gives addons no combat log, and some pages
  still wait for Forever data.
- The Binding of Illidan and the Raid Trainer games are gone.
- **In-game updates are off.** We do not have our own signing keys yet, so every new version is a new zip on this
  page.

## Versions

Versions restart for Forever: test builds are **0.x** (this is 0.1.0), go-live will be **1.0.0**. Copies from before
this build said "1.5.5"; DisasterZone treats that as older than every 0.x build, so nobody is told to "update" to it.
Everyone in the guild should run the same version: `/dz version` shows who runs what.

## Something wrong?

Report it in game: **`/dz bug`** (the last Lua error goes with it), **`/dz idea`** for ideas, **`/dz feedback`** to see
every report and its answers. Reports travel through the guild, so they arrive when someone who holds them is online.
Say what you were doing and where.
