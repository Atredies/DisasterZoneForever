# DisasterZone 0.2.2 for WoW Forever (sync you can see)

An update for **&lt;Casual Disaster&gt;**'s guild addon on **WoW Forever** (client 1.60.1, Interface 16001).
**Everyone should update, especially from 0.2.0:** a 0.2.0 copy can't read the BiS list of anyone who wears or
picked an item from Forever's new dungeons, so their lists never reach a 0.2.0 officer. `/dz version` shows who runs
what.

## Install / update

Step-by-step guide with pictures of the folder layout:
**[README](https://github.com/Atredies/DisasterZoneForever#readme)**. In short:

1. Download **`DisasterZone-0.2.2.zip`** below (under *Assets*).
2. Close the game.
3. Delete the old `DisasterZone*` folders from `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`, then unzip the new zip
   there, so the `DisasterZone` folder sits directly in `AddOns` (`...\AddOns\DisasterZone\DisasterZone.toc`).
4. Start the game. On the character screen, check that DisasterZone is ticked under **AddOns**. In game,
   `/dz version` shows which version everyone runs.

Your settings, BiS lists, recruitment message and game progress are kept (they live in your saved variables, not in
these folders).

## What changed since 0.2.0

0.2.1 was built but never published; 0.2.2 has everything from it.

- **Items from Forever's new dungeons work everywhere** (from 0.2.1). Their item numbers are higher than any TBC
  item, and 0.2.0 refused them: `/dz roll [item]` printed its help line, My BiS would not take them, and the loot
  log, council and **sync dropped them**: a whole BiS list with one such item was thrown away.
- **Sync says when it can't read a list.** Before, the list was dropped without a word. Now you see
  "Couldn't take X's BiS list (...). If they run a newer DisasterZone than you (/dz version), please update."
- **Recruitment message.** Trade and other channels get it without raid icons (the game doesn't draw them
  there; you saw `{rt1}` as text); Say and Yell keep them. GuildRecruitment is off by default, and the skip line
  tells you to `/join GuildRecruitment`.
- **List a group > Dungeon fits the window** (from 0.2.1): six tiles per level band, short names (BRD, LBRS,
  DM: East...) with the full name in the tooltip, and every level band has its picture.

## Hidden on Forever (for now)

- Gear sim, My Log, the strategy boards, meters and boss mods: Forever gives addons no combat log.
- **In-game updates are off.** We do not have our own signing keys yet, so every new version is a new zip on this
  page.

## Something wrong?

Report it in game: **`/dz bug`** (the last Lua error goes with it), **`/dz idea`** for ideas, **`/dz feedback`** to see
every report and its answers. Say what you were doing and where.
