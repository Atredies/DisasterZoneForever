# DisasterZone 0.2.0 for WoW Forever (second test build)

The second test build of **&lt;Casual Disaster&gt;**'s guild addon for **WoW Forever** (client 1.60.1, Interface 16001).
This one is for testing together: please run it with two or three guildmates (see the checklist below) and report
what breaks.

## Install

Step-by-step install guide with pictures of the folder layout, plus update and troubleshooting tips:
**[README](https://github.com/Atredies/DisasterZoneForever#readme)**. In short:

1. Download **`DisasterZone-0.2.0.zip`** below (under *Assets*).
2. Close the game.
3. Delete the old `DisasterZone*` folders from `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns` (if you had 0.1.0), then
   unzip the new zip there, so the `DisasterZone` folder sits directly in `AddOns`
   (`...\AddOns\DisasterZone\DisasterZone.toc`).
4. Start the game again. On the character screen, check that DisasterZone is ticked under **AddOns**. In game,
   `/dz` opens it and `/dz version` shows which version everyone runs.

The zip holds:

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `DisasterZone` | the addon (required) |
| `DisasterZone_Rooms` | the chess hall's 3D board (needed by Chess) |
| `DisasterZone_Bingo` | Council Bingo |
| `DisasterZone_Azeromon` | Azeromon |
| `DisasterZone_HoldTheLine` | Hold the Line |
| `DisasterZone_Chess` | Karazhan Chess |

The games are optional: leave out the folders you don't want. Settings and game progress are kept when you update
(they live in your saved variables, not in these folders).

## What changed since 0.1.0

- **Security fixes for the guild's data.** Events, sign-ups, polls, the council lists, officer notes and the other
  shared records only change when someone who may change them did it. Records passed on by another member say who
  passed them on, and a forged or relayed change from someone without the rights is refused.
- **Duplicate drops.** Two copies of the same item in one drop are rolled and awarded one by one: under soft
  reserves each copy goes to a different reserver, and the loot log records both copies, not one.
- **Sync after a big login.** When most of the raid logs in at the same time, everyone still ends up with every event,
  sign-up and list. Before, a few members could miss some until they reloaded.
- **The roll window comes back after a /reload.** If you reload while a roll is open, the roll window comes back with
  the time that is left (your `/roll` always counted; now you can see the roll again too).
- **Raid-night fixes.** Fixes from full simulated raids of Onyxia's Lair (40) and Hyjal Summit (20),
  for example sign-ups that got lost when they arrived before their event. Those test raids also cover a leader or
  master-looter change mid-raid, a disconnect during a roll and a /reload during a boss pull.
- **Forever, not TBC.** Azeromon's Guild Hall now has wings for Onyxia's Lair, Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit (the TBC
  raid names are gone), and items for one class only ("Classes: Warrior" on the tooltip) are treated that way in loot
  decisions.

## Multi-player test checklist

For two or three testers, at least one of them an officer. Tick what works, and report anything that doesn't.

1. Everyone logs in with 0.2.0. `/dz version` lists all of you on 0.2.0.
2. Open **Members**: every tester sees the others online.
3. The officer creates an **event** and a **raid** (Raids page). Everyone else sees both within a minute.
4. The other testers **sign up** for the event and the raid. The officer sees each sign-up.
5. The officer starts a **poll**; everyone votes; everyone sees the same result.
6. Everyone picks a few items in **My BiS**. The officer sees your lists in the loot council view (**BiS sync**).
7. **Form a raid** (a party converted to a raid is enough) and start a raid session.
8. The officer (master looter) starts a **roll**. If you can, test with **two identical items** in one loot: each copy
   should get its own roll and its own winner.
9. While a roll is open, one tester types **`/reload`**: the roll window should come back for them, and their roll
   should count.
10. Anything wrong: **`/dz bug`** right away (the last Lua error goes with it). Say what you were doing.

## Hidden on Forever (for now)

- Gear sim, My Log, the strategy boards, meters and boss mods: Forever gives addons no combat log.
- **In-game updates are off.** We do not have our own signing keys yet, so every new version is a new zip on this
  page.

## Something wrong?

Report it in game: **`/dz bug`** (the last Lua error goes with it), **`/dz idea`** for ideas, **`/dz feedback`** to see
every report and its answers. Reports travel through the guild, so they arrive when someone who holds them is online.
Say what you were doing and where.
