# DisasterZone: third-party notices

DisasterZone for WoW Forever ships two kinds of third-party material:

- open-source libraries, in `Libs\`
- item data that was generated from other addons' data, in `Data\`

Section 1 was checked against the files in this folder on 2026-09-28, for the TBC original. Section 2
describes the Forever data as of 2026-11-09.
Keep this file, and the license files inside `Libs\`, whenever you pass DisasterZone on.

This file is a list of attributions and known license terms. It is not legal advice.

## 2. Generated item data (`Data\`)

This is the WoW Forever version of DisasterZone (client 1.60.1). Its item data is Forever's and was generated by
scripts in the dev kit (`dev/generators/`), which are not part of the addon. Each file's header lists its exact inputs.
The TBC Anniversary data of the original DisasterZone is no longer shipped; the dev kit keeps it in `dev/fixtures/tbc/`
for its tests.

| File | Contents | Generator |
|---|---|---|
| `Data\Items.lua` | Forever's raids and their bosses. Raid loot is added once it is known (the raids open on 9 December 2026). No tier tokens. | `gen_forever_data.py` |
| `Data\Dungeons.lua` | Forever's 35 dungeons, bosses and drops (one difficulty) | `gen_forever_data.py` |
| `Data\Sources.lua` | Crafted gear, reputation rewards and PvP gear | `gen_forever_data.py` |
| `Data\Atlas.lua`, `Data\AtlasClassic.lua` | The Atlas tab: recipes and crafts, reputation, PvP, world event and set pages, and the item names for its search | `gen_forever_atlas.py`, `gen_atlas_classic.py` |
| `Data\Gems.lua` | No sockets or gems on Forever. Enchant records for the enchants AtlasLoot Classic Forever lists, taken by name from the TBC file's records (WoWSims TBC data) | `gen_forever_gems.py` |
| `Data\ItemClasses.lua` | Still the TBC Anniversary file (class-restricted items, from AtlasLootClassic's `Requirements.lua`). Mostly inert on Forever; it will be regenerated. | `gen_itemclasses.py` |
| `Data\BiSLists.lua` | Empty on Forever: no suggested BiS lists yet | - |

`Data\ForeverRaids.lua` (the raid planner's raids) and `Data\Consumables.lua` (the level-60 consumables kit) are written
by hand from what the game shows and from Forever's item database.

### Main source: AtlasLoot Classic Forever (GNU GPL v2)

`Items.lua`, `Dungeons.lua`, `Sources.lua`, `Atlas.lua` and `AtlasClassic.lua` are derived mainly from **AtlasLoot
Classic Forever** (CurseForge project 1422985), the WoW Forever edition of AtlasLootClassic (by Okrah and Lag). Its modules
declare `## X-License: GPL v2`, and AtlasLootClassic ships the full text of the **GNU General Public License,
Version 2, June 1991** in its `LICENSE` file.

### Other inputs

| Source | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Wowhead** (Forever item tooltips, `nether.wowhead.com/forever`) | Every shipped item ID is checked against Wowhead's Forever item database; an item it doesn't know is left out. The answers are cached in the dev kit. No Wowhead text or files are shipped. |
| **Public Forever databases** (foreverchanges.pro, wowf.io, wowforevertalents.com, forever.warcraftdb.com) | Dungeons and bosses AtlasLoot Classic Forever doesn't have yet: names, levels, bosses and the item IDs of the new dungeons. Facts only; the sources are listed per dungeon in `dev/generators/data/forever_dungeon_loot_extra.json`. |
| **The game client** | Raid names, instance IDs and bosses recorded with `/dz raiddata`, and items seen in game |

### Data from the TBC original

The TBC Anniversary DisasterZone also used numeric data from Deadly Boss Mods, Questie, LootReserve and LoonBestInSlot
(boss IDs, item classes, BiS lists). That data is in `dev/fixtures/tbc/`, not in this addon. The one TBC data file
still shipped, `Data\ItemClasses.lua`, comes from AtlasLootClassic (GPL v2).

### GPL terms may apply when DisasterZone is redistributed

`Data\Items.lua`, `Data\Dungeons.lua`, `Data\Sources.lua`, `Data\ItemClasses.lua`, `Data\Atlas.lua` and
`Data\AtlasClassic.lua` are derived from GPL v2 data. If DisasterZone is redistributed (posted publicly, uploaded to
CurseForge, Wago or GitHub, or handed to people outside the guild), the **GPL v2 may apply to those files, and possibly
to the addon as a whole** when shipped with them. That can mean:

- including a copy of the GPL v2 text (copy AtlasLootClassic's `LICENSE`; DisasterZone does not ship one yet);
- keeping the attribution in this file and in the data file headers;
- giving recipients the same rights under the GPL, and adding no further restrictions;
- making the source available. The Lua files are the source; the generator scripts are in the dev kit.

Whether bare game IDs and drop tables are protected by copyright at all is unclear. Before a public release, decide
whether to accept the GPL for DisasterZone, or to ship without these files and generate them locally.

## 5. Strategy board raid maps and boss portraits

On WoW Forever the strategy boards are hidden, and `Data\RaidMaps.lua`, `Data\BossAbilities.lua` and
`Data\BossPortraits.lua` hold no data yet (the TBC data is in the dev kit's `dev/fixtures/tbc/`). The TBC raid map
textures below still ship in `Media\Maps\` until they are removed. The notes describe the TBC original.

**Raid maps (`Media\Maps\*.blp`).** These are Blizzard Entertainment's own floor maps of the TBC raids (the in-game world map art of later clients). The TBC Anniversary client has none. They were downloaded from wowhead's image server (wow.zamimg.com, `maps/enus/original/<zone>-<floor>.jpg`) and converted to DXT1 BLP textures by `dev/generators/gen_raid_maps.py`. They are © Blizzard Entertainment. They are not openly licensed: they ship here the same way addons commonly reuse game art, for use inside World of Warcraft only. Don't redistribute them outside that context. `dev/art/blizzard_maps/` in the dev kit keeps the downloaded originals.

**Boss abilities (`Data\BossAbilities.lua`).** Only numbers: spell IDs from the installed DBM-Raids-BC modules, and for each one whether players can remove it (its dispel type: Magic, Curse, Poison, Disease, Enrage) as listed on wowhead.com. Names and icons come from the game client.

**Boss portraits (`Data\BossPortraits.lua`).** Only numbers and names: creature IDs from Questie's database (GPL-3.0) and creature display IDs as listed on wowhead.com. The portraits themselves are drawn by the game client from its own models; no Blizzard art is shipped.

The shapes of the strategy board (`Media\Strategy\disc.tga`, `ring.tga`, `arrow.tga`) are drawn by `dev/generators/gen_strategy_media.py`.
